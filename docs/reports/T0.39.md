# T0.39 — Revert only on a wrong answer, never on silence

The deploy check read a site that never answered as a site that was down, and down reverts the merge
at main's tip. On 2026-09-24 the machine running the schedule could not look aeni.ma up, and only a
run's own judgement kept it from reverting a healthy merge. `health.mjs` now reads three outcomes —
up, down and unknown — and only down reverts; unknown records nothing and the next run asks again.

## ACs implemented

| AC | What was built | Its test |
|---|---|---|
| AC1 | `assess` keeps a silence (`status: null`) apart from a wrong status: `failed` holds wrong answers alone, `unanswered` the silences, and `outcome` is **unknown** when a check never answered and none answered wrongly. Unknown carries no revert signal — the old `ok` field, which the skill used to read `false` from, is gone, and the skill's step e reverts on `outcome: down` alone. The deploy-window wait reports `outcome: null`, since nothing was asked. | TC1 — `scripts/run/health.test.mjs`, "is unknown, never down, when a check never answered…", "is up when every check answered…", "reads a site that never answered as unknown…", "waits, asking and recording nothing…" |
| AC2 | A wrong status is **down** whether or not another check was silent; `health` records the commit, so one outage reverts one merge, and prints `failedText` for the Fix task. | TC2 — "is down on a wrong status, whether or not another check was silent", "records a failed commit too…" |
| AC3 | On unknown, `health` returns before writing `aenima-deploy-checked`, with a `why` saying the site could not be reached from this machine and is asked again next run. | TC3 — "reads a site that never answered as unknown, records nothing, and asks again next run" |
| AC4 | `.claude/skills/ticket/SKILL.md` step e branches on `outcome: up`, `outcome: unknown` and `outcome: down`; `docs/guidelines.md` §5's step-0 row and its *deploy check* paragraph name up, down and unknown and say only down reverts; v1.28 header line. `scripts/run/README.md` says the same. | TC4 — "the skill's step e reverts on down alone and asks again on unknown", "the guidelines' deploy check names up, down and unknown, and only down reverts" |
