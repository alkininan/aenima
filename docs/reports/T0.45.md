## Out of scope

- **`branch.mjs` cannot carry the planner's ticket file across a reused branch.** On a second
  claim — an addendum round, a Decision answered, a migration applied — the planner writes
  `docs/tickets/<id>.md` in a worktree standing at `origin/main`, where that path is untracked,
  and step 3 then runs `git checkout -B t<id> origin/t<id>` against a branch that tracks the same

## Defaults taken

Each a choice the ticket and design-spec v2.21 leave open, cheap to undo, taken to keep
building:

- **TR and NL are partial dictionaries.** `src/i18n/tr.ts` and `nl.ts` carry `shortcuts` and
  `common.close` / `common.skipToContent`, merged over English in `index.ts`; `Dictionary` is
  now the string-widened shape of `en` (`en` stays `as const` for the laws script). The ticket
  asks for the sheet's rows in three languages and no full dictionary exists; a partial is the
  shape `index.ts` anticipated, and moves wholesale into the full ones when they land.
- **The account menu is named by the signed-in address** — the trigger's own text, as the
  menu's `aria-label` — and the avatar beside it is `aria-hidden`, so the trigger's name is the
  address alone. §4 gives the slot no name of its own.
- **Key caps.** One pill per chord; on Apple platforms the modifier glyph stands beside the key
  (`⌘Z`, `⇧↑`), elsewhere a plus joins words (`Ctrl+Z`, `Shift+↑`); arrows print as `↑` and
  `↓`, both in the vendored JetBrains Mono subset; Enter, Esc, Tab, Space, Home and End as the
  keyboard prints them. §8.15 names only the modifier's two forms.
- **"Recorded keyboard input" is a latch.** The modality script writes `data-keyboard-recorded`
  on `<html>` at the first focus key and never removes it; the hint's stylesheet rule and the
  row's hook read that, not the modality of the moment — which flips back to `pointer` at the
  next tap, and an iPad with a keyboard taps and types by turns.
- **`/` focuses the page's `input[type="search"]`** where one exists and is otherwise left to
  the browser; the sheet carries no row for it — the Decision named no phrase for it and no
  product page has a search yet (§8.29's filter bar).
- **A route change is a change of pathname.** A change of search alone — a filter, a page —
  leaves focus where it is, so a keyboard user who chose a filter keeps their place in the
  strip. The way back is keyed on pathname and search both, so a filtered list is its own place.
- **The main region is the `/app` layout's** (`AppShell`, `id="content"`), and the page and its
  `loading.tsx` render a content column inside it — the element focused after a route change
  has to survive the skeleton's replacement. `/i/[key]`, `/o/[key]`, `/` and `/sign-in` keep
  their own `<main>`, each with `tabindex="-1"`.
- **`RouteFocus` leaves focus alone** on a fresh load — §11 starts one at the skip link — and
  when the arriving page has already placed focus inside the main region.
- **The way back's record is the row's link, and the menu's "Open".** `data-row-link` carries
  the row's key; a click or an Enter on the link records it through `RouteFocus`, a modifier
  click records nothing. The overflow menu's "Open" is not that link, so it records the same
  place itself before it navigates — the fix round's Must; the first build left it unrecorded.
- **`ItemRowData.href` is handed in by the caller** — `itemHref(key)` on `/app`,
  `/dev/item?row=<key>` in the fixture — so the `/dev/list` mirror navigates to `/dev/item`,
  which gained the real page's "Back to the list" link, pointed at `/dev/list`. The mirror was
  already the shell's one browser-reachable render; now it is the shell.
- **The sheet reads the page's shape once, at open:** a list is `[data-row-link]`, the grid
  `[role="grid"]`, panels `[aria-haspopup], [role="combobox"]`. A group with nothing on the page
  is omitted. "Jump to the first or last" stands in Lists and in Panels; "Move within a row"
  appears once the list is the grid; Chat and Drag have no rows until the dock and a reordering
  list exist.
- **The skip link is `buttonClasses` Neutral sm plus one unlayered `.skip-link` rule** — fixed
  at 16/16, `--z-tooltip`, a 1×1 clipped box while not focused. Unlayered because a rule in
  `@layer components` loses to the utility sizes it has to undo whatever its specificity, the
  reason the reduced-motion block already stands outside every layer; the kbd's touch rule
  stands there for the same reason.
- **`Kbd` answers `Ctrl` on the server and corrects after hydration.** Only the kitchen-sink
  route renders a hint on the server; every product hint opens on the client.
- **The kbd's 20 height holds `py-[2px]`** and the 1px bottom border around a 16 line, centred
  by flex — §8.15's "pad 2/6, 20h" as written.
- **Sign out submits a hidden POST form** (`requestSubmit`) from its row; the route handler is
  a POST and stays one.
- **The browser checks pass over `next dev`'s own overlay stop** (`nextjs-portal`), as
  `item.spec.ts` does; the product ships no such stop.
- **Sink additions for the checks:** a §8.15 Kbd hint section, `type="search"` on the Search
  field, and a text `Input` inside the content modal.
