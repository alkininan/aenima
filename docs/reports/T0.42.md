# T0.42 — Forms and auth to v2.21

**Branch:** `t0-42` · **Spec:** design-spec v2.21 §7, §8.1–§8.4 · §17 C-07, C-08, C-18, C-19, C-27, C-31
· drift at claim: cited 2.21, repo 2.23 (`version-drift.mjs`)

The sign-in step and the form primitives brought to v2.21. The floated label now moves on
`transform` alone: at rest it is ui-label scaled 17/13 from its top-left, and its box is only as
wide as its text, so the scale cannot push a 375 page sideways. The OTP group reserves two helper
lines. A field in error keeps its danger border and icon when focused. Icon buttons declare their
(box − icon) ÷ 2 padding. OTP boxes use `--r-pill`. Every control gets `touch-action: manipulation`,
and the tap highlight is transparent. The step uses §8.3's geometry: mark 48 from the top (16 below
600 tall), 24 to the title block, 24 to the first label zone, a 400 column inside the mode's gutters,
and 8 from the primary to a Neutral md tertiary. The resend keeps its label `--n-secondary` while it
counts down, with the time in a mono-readout span. Request-level messages (a rate limit, an outage,
a failed send) now land in one slot under the step's last control, not on a field's helper line.

## Open questions

1. **Field block → primary.** §8.3 says field blocks stack with no further gap, then "Controls
   follow, then the primary". It does not say how far the primary sits below the last helper line.
   The sign-in form keeps its existing 16. Owner: design-spec.
2. **OTP mechanics.** v2.21 §8.2 describes "one logical input behind six boxes", and a wrong code
   that selects all six digits so the next keystroke replaces them. `OtpInput` is still six inputs
   and keeps the digits unselected. None of this ticket's checks (C-07, C-08, C-18, C-19, C-27,
   C-31) or its Objective names it, so it was left alone. Owner: T-next.
