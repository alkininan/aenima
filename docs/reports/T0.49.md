## Open questions

1. **AC1's 792 case.** The board's AC1 lists "at 792 and 791 (hand, box 760 and 759)" under two lines of 72; §8.27 puts the hand-chrome break "narrower than 792" and C-12 reads "one line in a content box of 760 or more", and the engine draws 56 at 792. The build follows the spec's rule and TC1 pins 792 at 56 beside 1048; if the AC's wording was meant literally, the threshold would have to be 761, which no cited section supports — the AC line reads as a slip in the ticket's own arithmetic rather than a rule. The reviewer's to confirm.
2. **The bucket header in the grid's tree.** `RowWalker` is the `grid` and each bucket's rows are a `rowgroup` named by its header; the `<section>` and its `<h2>` stand between them, which ARIA's grid model does not list among a grid's owned elements (rows, rowgroups, or generic wrappers). Screen readers tolerate it; a later accessibility pass could fold the header into the rowgroup as a captioning row. Not this ticket's, which names the three roles and nothing about headers.
3. **The freshness column's inner alignment.** The column is the trailing column at both heights and its content — dot, readout or Park — leads inside the 72, so the dots align down the list as a table's would; §8.27's "with the freshness readout at the trailing edge" is read as the column's place. If it was meant as flush-right text, it is one utility on the cell.
4. **§8.27's landing mechanism is engine-true only on a full navigation.** The ticket's reading — Chromium reveals a closed `<details>` ancestor on fragment navigation — holds for a typed or reloaded URL, and not for the app router's client-side navigation from the list, which scrolls by `scrollIntoView()` and reveals nothing (observed in TC4 before the fallback went in). The chip now carries `?check={id}` as well, and the page opens the panel on it. A line in design-spec §8.27 saying the link names the check in the query as well as the fragment would keep the next builder from re-deriving this; a spec edit is yours, not a run's.
5. **The budget spec's row locator.** Its first attempt on this tree resolved "Weekly digest email" to two `item-row`s — the sink's list surface renders the same titles — and the retry passed; the locator is now scoped to the fixture list's own links (`href="/dev/item"`). Why a sink row was still in the DOM in the frame after the first route change was not chased: C-45's retry-once is by design and the p75 was 32 ms.
