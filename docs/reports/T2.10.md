## Defaults taken

Four, none of them expensive to undo, all recorded here and in the claim's one board comment:

- **The notice lives on the `not-asked` check line in the meter's expansion, not as a card in the
  gap list.** Open question 14 says "beside the move that argues applicability", and §5's first
  move does not exist yet, so there was no control to sit beside. The expansion is where §4's
  renormalization already speaks and where §1 law 3's interrogation lives; the gap list is what
  §13 says an item *owes*, and a closed gap owes nothing. Undoing it is moving one JSX block.
- **It is gated on the run's own `not-asked` state**, which makes it a claim about the denominator
  now rather than about history.
- **The reason is read from the ledger, not added to `gap`.** The schema's doctrine is explicit
  that a closed row carries a time, no name and no note, and that why it closed is a ledger fact.
- **The `review-scope.mjs` Fix went to E0.2 Pipeline, not to this ticket's E2.1 Scoring engine.**
  §5 step 5 says an out-of-scope finding inherits the Epic; a `scripts/run/**` bug filed under the
  scoring epic is a misfiling a human would have to correct, and the skill itself names E0.2 for
  pipeline reds. Moving one relation undoes it.

## Open questions

1. **The item page now makes two unindexed scans of `activity`.** Nothing covers
   `(subject_table, subject_id)`; the feed read has carried that note since T1.3 and this read
   adds a second one for every item holding a closed gap. One index answers both. Fine at this
   size and wrong at some larger one. **Owner: T-next**, whenever ledger size first shows up in a
   page's timing.
2. **Only the latest run's checks can carry the notice.** The page renders one run, and an item
   scored against two packs — a PRD and a design package — shows whichever ran last. A gap closed
   on the other pack's check has no line to sit on until that pack's run is the latest. This is
   the page's existing shape rather than something this ticket introduced, and it disappears when
   the page renders more than one artifact's run. **Owner: T-next.**
3. **The notice has no control, by design.** A person who disagrees can read the closure and
   nothing else — `reopen_gap` moves `accepted` back and a closed gap is terminal. §5's first
   negotiation move, "doesn't apply here", is Phase 3 and lands on this same line; until it
   ships the answer to "is that right?" is a conversation, not a button. **Owner: Phase 3.**
