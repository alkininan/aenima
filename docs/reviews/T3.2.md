   (`docs/reports/T3.2.md:24`) reads "1 failed / 11 passed", and the named mutation — `run.ts`
   handing the loop `baseSectionHash: null` always — reddens only *opens the next cycle once the
   human has rewritten that section*. `run.test.ts:278` (*keeps the question closed while the
