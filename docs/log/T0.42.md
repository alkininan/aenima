# T0.42 — Forms and auth to v2.21
_2026-09-25T08:58:30Z_

The sign-in step and the form primitives now match design-spec v2.21. The floated label moves on
`transform` alone: at rest it is its floated ui-label self, scaled 17/13 from its top-left, with the
x offset giving back the scaled zone padding so the glyphs still sit where the value will. The OTP
group reserves two helper lines, a field in error keeps its danger border and icon under focus, icon
buttons declare their (box − icon) ÷ 2 padding, OTP boxes are `--r-pill`, and every control is
`touch-action: manipulation` with a transparent tap highlight. The step has §8.3's geometry, inside
§4's hand-chrome gutters of 16 below 1024. The resend is Neutral md; while it counts down its label
stays `--n-secondary` and the clock sits in a mono-readout span. Rate limits and outages leave the
field helpers and land in one request-level slot under the step's last control.

Two lessons cost something. The label's box had been the zone's full width, which is harmless
unscaled. Scaled 17/13 it overshot the field and gave a 375 page a scrollWidth of 490. The review
caught it, and the box now hugs its text. Second, the countdown's parts, a sentence around a mono
span, became three flex items inside the Button's `inline-flex` row, gapped apart and spaced in the
accessible name, until they were wrapped in one inline span. In the tests, a verify held open to
observe an in-flight state has to be settled in a `finally`: React 19 entangles async transitions,
and one left pending keeps every later test's `isPending` true.

Open: §8.3 does not name the gap from the last field block to the primary (16 kept), and §8.2's
"one logical input behind six boxes" OTP mechanics are not built.
