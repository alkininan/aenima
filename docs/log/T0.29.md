Two lines in v2.21 said something the rest of the document denied, and the changelog had stopped
recording. v2.22 settles all three.

The first was §2's comment on `--hero-gradient`: *marketing surfaces + 100%-meter shimmer only*.
§6 fires that shimmer when the meter reaches handover-ready, and §8.23 spells out that
handover-ready means every Must passed or accepted **whatever the percentage reads** — a meter can
sit at 84 and be there. The comment tied the gradient to a number the body had already let go of,
and under the preface's precedence order a comment that contradicts the body is a stop, not a
detail. It now names the state: *the handover-ready shimmer (§6) only*.

The second was C-39, which asked that *every shipped woff2 covers ğĞşŞİıĳĲ*. No correct build can
satisfy that, because §3 splits every face into a Latin and a Latin Extended subset and gives each
its own file, and those eight letters all live in the second — so the Latin file of a face that is
entirely right fails a file-by-file reading. §3 already said the check reads every shipped *face*,
and `scripts/fonts/check_fonts.py` already implements it that way, with the reason in its own
docstring. The check's sentence was the last copy still saying file. It now reads *each shipped
face covers ğĞşŞİıĳĲ across its subset files*, and nothing in the code follows from it — the
document caught up with the check, not the other way round.

The third was the changelog itself. v2.19, v2.20 and v2.21 each landed changes with their reasons
in the body and no line in the header comment, and a reader who takes the comment as the list of
what changed would not know to test them. The v2.22 line carries them: the dimming steps, the
meter turning `--success` at handover-ready, the enter-over-`--t-med` timings, the row's one gap
chip, undo living in the toast, when a control may be disabled, the floated label's scale, the
doc-heading ladder, `Cmd/Ctrl+K` going quiet under a modal, and the rest.

The review found the shape of the first problem twice more. §2's accent table still ties
`--success` to *meter at 100*, twelve lines below the comment this ticket corrected, and

no check between the two. Neither is one of the five replacements, and the ticket's Rules say a
check that does not pass is reported and never patched in the spec, so both are reported and
filed. A third finding is older than this ticket: §17's C-01 to C-04 say `(grep)` and `(script)`
