# T0.37 — Morph menus and selects
_2026-09-24T11:11:38Z_

Menus, selects, toasts and tooltips now follow design-spec §6's morph: a panel is a popover in the
top layer, grown out of the control that opened it through one same-document view transition, placed
by one rule that anchor positioning and the JS positioner share, and closed at once on every path.
Toasts are one at a time, bottom-centre, with `Cmd/Ctrl+Z` on a showing undo and a host that moves
into an open modal; only panels and toasts carry a blur; and the kitchen-sink route runs the
fallback paths in one engine behind `?fallback=1`.

The first claim stopped at Decision on three of §6's own sentences. In Chromium an anchor hidden by
`visibility` stops its anchor-positioned panel taking pointer events under the default
`position-visibility`, which §6 forbids setting — so every menu and select was dead to the mouse on
that path while the suite stayed green, because the press checks that found it were `fixme`. The
answer was *default*: the trigger hides by `opacity: 0` and `pointer-events: none`, and the spec
patch is its own task. Review then found two things no green test could see, both measured in the
engine: the toast region, shown as a popover, took the UA's `inset: 0` and `Canvas` fill and sat at
the top-left; and the contents' `view-transition-name` sat on a `display: contents` wrapper that
capture skips, so they popped in rather than faded. Both now have browser assertions that go red,
and the three lessons are filed in `.claude/rules/surfaces.md`.
