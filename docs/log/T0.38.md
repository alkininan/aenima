`scripts/design/spec-checks.mjs` runs C-01–C-04 over the design spec, and `scripts/design/laws.mjs`
reads the stylesheet and every class string from the syntax tree for C-06 and C-39's literal
clause, the dictionary for C-33 and C-34, the four allowlists for C-35–C-38, and the scrollbar

dashboard", the spaced freshness units, "Reopen", and the unclear and accepted gap chips). The
